I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Beijing Institute of Technology.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

My research focuses on the mathematical analysis of kinetic equations, with particular emphasis on the Vlasov–Poisson systems and related self-consistent field models.

My work mainly concerns the long-time behavior of kinetic models, including:

- propagation of moments and velocity support;
- conservation laws and energy methods;
- stochastic perturbations of kinetic equations.

My research aims to develop analytical methods for kinetic equations, focusing on the interplay between nonlinear dynamics, singular interactions, conservation laws, and stochastic effects.
