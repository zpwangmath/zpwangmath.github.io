I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at Beijing Institute of Technology.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

My research focuses on the mathematical analysis of kinetic equations, especially the Vlasov–Poisson systems and related models.

My work concerns moment propagation, velocity support, conservation laws, energy methods, and stochastic perturbations of kinetic equations.
